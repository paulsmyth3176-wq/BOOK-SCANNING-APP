    Add a book by hand

    For books with no barcode (older books, gifts, signed copies).

    Backup & restore

    Your list is stored on this phone only. Save a backup now and then (to Google Drive, email, etc.).

    Restore or import

    Accepts a ShelfScan backup, or a CSV export from Goodreads, Libib, Bookshelf, LibraryThing, or a spreadsheet with an ISBN column. Duplicates are skipped.

    Install on your phone

    In Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen → Install. In Samsung Internet: ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen. Once installed it opens like a regular app and works without signal in the store.
